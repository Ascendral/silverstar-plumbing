Delta & East County

Brentwood, Discovery Bay & Oakley

Delta-area homes have their own patterns: hard water chewing through heaters and fixtures, older poly and galvanized lines on the original tracts, and irrigation and slab leaks that show up as a water bill before they show up on a floor.

We do a lot of softener installs, water heater swaps, repipes and leak location out here — including waterfront and second homes, where an automatic shut-off valve pays for itself the first time you're away.

Stanislaus County

Patterson & the Valley

Patterson and the surrounding Valley towns run hard water and long hot summers, which is rough on tanks, tankless heat exchangers, and hose bibs.

Softeners, reverse osmosis drinking water, water heater replacement and gas line work for ranges, dryers and outdoor kitchens are the everyday jobs here.
